Fly Fishing Around Spokane

Chasing trout in Eastern Washington

One of my favorite things to do is fly fishing around Spokane. You don't have to drive far to find good water, from the river that runs right through town to quiet lakes out in the scablands.

The Spokane River is home to native redband trout, and on summer evenings the caddis hatches can get the fish rising everywhere. Being out on the water with a fly rod is the best way I know to slow down and clear my head.

My favorite spots

  • Spokane River - redband trout right in town
  • Little Spokane River - small, quiet water north of the city
  • Amber Lake - selective gear rules and big rainbows
  • Rocky Ford Creek - a fly-fishing-only spring creek
